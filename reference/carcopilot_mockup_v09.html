Here's what I'm seeing
thinking on-device…
Cylinder 1 keeps misfiring — your O2 sensor is reading rich and idle is rough. On Corollas this age, that pattern almost always means a worn ignition coil. Each cylinder has its own little coil pack, and when one starts going, you get exactly this combo.
The good news: it's a 30-minute fix with one $45 part. I can walk you through it.

Find the coils
thinking on-device…
Pop the hood. You're looking for four small black rectangular blocks sitting on top of the engine, in a row. Each one has a single wire connector running to it. Those are the ignition coils. Cylinder 1 is closest to the timing belt side — that's the side facing the front of the car.

What to do — in order
1
Put your hazards on and find a place to stop. Don't worry about the perfect spot — a shoulder, parking lot, or side street is fine.
2
Turn heat to max and the fan to high. I know it's weird — heat pulls warmth out of the engine and into the cabin. Buys you a couple miles.
3
Once stopped: engine off, hood up. Don't touch the radiator cap — it's pressurized and will scald you. Wait 30 minutes before doing anything else, then I'll check the readings.
